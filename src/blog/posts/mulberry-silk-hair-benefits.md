Walk into any beauty aisle and you'll find satin pillowcases, silk scrunchies, and silk-wrapped accessories everywhere. But there's a reason LUME uses specifically **mulberry silk**—and it's not just a marketing distinction.

## What Makes Mulberry Silk Different

Mulberry silk comes from silkworms fed exclusively on mulberry leaves. The result is a fiber that's remarkably uniform in diameter and exceptionally smooth at the microscopic level. Compared to charmeuse, habotai, or blended "silk-like" synthetics, mulberry silk has:

- **A tighter, more consistent weave** that eliminates micro-friction against the hair shaft
- **Natural protein structure (fibroin)** that is chemically similar to keratin—your hair's own building block
- **Moisture-regulating properties** that reduce overnight dryness without leaving a greasy residue

Satin (which is a weave, not a material) is often polyester. It's slippery, yes, but it also creates static, doesn't breathe, and doesn't have any of silk's natural protein benefits.

## The Friction Problem

Hair breakage is mostly a friction problem. Every time strands rub against a rough surface—a cotton pillowcase, a rubber band, a plastic roller—the cuticle scales lift and snag. Over time this leads to the split ends and frizz that feel impossible to fix.

Mulberry silk's smooth surface gives hair nothing to catch on. Strands slide freely, cuticles stay flat, and moisture stays inside the shaft where it belongs.

## For Color-Treated and Processed Hair

If your hair is bleached, chemically straightened, or heavily processed, the outer cuticle layer is already compromised. These hair types need protection even more than virgin hair does. Mulberry silk is gentle enough to use every single night without accelerating further damage.

## Beyond the Hair: Skin Benefits Too

Mulberry silk's temperature-regulating properties help keep your face cooler overnight, which is linked to reduced morning puffiness. And because silk absorbs far less moisture than cotton, your skincare products stay on your face rather than transferring to the curler.
