# Design Doc: LUME - One-Item E-commerce Startup

**Goal:** Launch a minimalist, "Organic Glow" e-commerce company selling Heatless Silk Hair Curler Sets, optimized for Pinterest/Facebook and apartment-based fulfillment.

## 1. Brand Concept: **LUME**
*   **Target Audience:** Women aged 18–45 interested in "clean girl" aesthetics, haircare, and time-saving beauty hacks.
*   **Visual Direction:** "The Organic Glow" (Minimalist, calm, approachable).
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   Primary: Warm Beige (`#F5F5DC`)
    *   Secondary: Soft Cream (`#FFFDD0`)
    *   Accent: Sage Green (`#B2AC88`)
*   **Typography:** Clean Sans-Serif (Inter) for body, Elegant Serif (Playfair Display) for headings.

## 2. Product: **LUME Original Heatless Curler Set**
*   **Components:** 1x Silk/Satin Curling Rod, 2x Scrunchies, 1x Claw Clip.
*   **Initial Batch:** 60 units.
*   **Pricing:**
    *   Unit Cost: $4.00 (estimated)
    *   Target Retail: $19.99
    *   Gross Profit: $15.99 (80% margin)

## 3. Technology Stack (Capital Efficient)
*   **Frontend:** Vite + React + Vanilla CSS (Aesthetics-first).
*   **Payments:** Stripe Checkout (One-page buy).
*   **Hosting:** Vercel (Free tier).
*   **Fulfillment:** PirateShip + USPS Ground Advantage.

## 4. Fulfillment Workflow
*   **Warehouse:** User's Apartment (Plastic Bin storage).
*   **Packaging:** 6x9 Sage Green Poly-mesh mailers.
*   **Labeling:** Automated CSV export from Stripe -> PirateShip.

## 5. Marketing Strategy
*   **Phase 1 (Setup):** Create 10 "GRWM" videos and 5 tutorial reels.
*   **Phase 2 (Launch):** Post on Pinterest (Search-optimized) and Facebook Marketplace/Ads.
*   **Phase 3 (Optimization):** Follow up for reviews and "Hair Reveal" galleries.

## 6. Success Metrics
*   **Primary:** Sell through first 60 units within 30 days.
*   **Secondary:** 15% conversion rate on landing page.
*   **Tertiary:** <$5 CAC (Cost per Acquisition) via Pinterest/Facebook.
