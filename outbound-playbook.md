Every sequence ladders up to these proof points. Use them deliberately — never more than 1-2 per email:

| Stat | Use when |
|------|----------|
| Sub-60s lead response (industry avg: 4.7 hours) | Opening contrast hook |
| $25/qualified lead vs $150+ human SDR | ROI / budget conversation |
| 3x more demos booked vs form-based capture | Demo volume / pipeline |
| 40% improvement in demo-to-close rate | Quality / conversion argument |
| 30-day free pilot, no credit card | Closing / low-commitment ask |
