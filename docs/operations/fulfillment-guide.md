# Fulfillment Guide: LUME Operations

Congratulations on launching LUME! This guide explains how to manage your physical inventory and fulfill orders from your apartment.

## 1. Inventory Management
- **Storage:** Keep your 60-100 units in a single, clean plastic bin (to prevent dust). 
- **Stock Check:** Manually count your units every Sunday to ensure your website "In Stock" message is accurate.

## 2. Supplies You Need
- **Poly Mailers:** 6x9 Sage Green or Cream (Eco-friendly preferred).
- **Label Printer:** A simple thermal printer (Phomemo or Brother) is best, but a regular printer with tape works too.
- **Thank You Cards:** Simple minimalist cards (Cream paper) with a handwritten "Thank you for glowing with us!" note.

## 3. Shipping Workflow (The Daily 15-Min Routine)
1.  **Export Orders:** Once a day, log into Stripe and export your new "Succeeded" orders to a CSV.
2.  **PirateShip Sync:** Upload the CSV to [PirateShip.com](https://pirateship.com) (Free account). It will automatically calculate the cheapest USPS Ground Advantage rates.
3.  **Print & Pack:** Print the labels, place the curler set and thank-you card in the poly-mailer, and stick the label on.
4.  **Drop Off:** Drop the batch at any USPS blue bin or post office on your way out.

## 4. Re-ordering (When to Scale)
- When you hit **15 units** remaining, place your next Alibaba order immediately (to account for the 10-14 day lead time).
- Consider ordering a "branded" poly-mailer for the next 500 units to level up the unboxing experience.
